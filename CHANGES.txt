2. STRUCTURE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Started as a single page; rebuilt as a multi-page site.

  Pages:  Home (/), Collection (/collection), Add (/add),
          About (/about), 404
  Shell:  RootLayout keeps nav/footer/background mounted across routes.
          Page transitions use useOutlet() + AnimatePresence so the
          outgoing page keeps rendering while it leaves.
  Store:  QuotesContext — one source of truth, same API whether data is
          local or shared.


3. HOME PAGE (evolved over several rounds)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Stripped to just the title, an "Add a quote" button, and a
  "Reveal the quotes" toggle. Tagline/eyebrow/extra sections removed.
- Every saved quote floats around the title as a bubble:
    * dormant  = heavily blurred, like shapes behind a foggy window
    * revealed = sharp and readable, fading in as a staggered cascade
- Bubbles are draggable — pick one up, drop it anywhere on the stage.
- Clicking a bubble does NOT navigate. It morphs in place into a large
  reading dialog via a shared Framer Motion layoutId, and morphs back
  into the same spot on close (close button, Escape, or backdrop).
- Revealing the quotes hides the title and buttons entirely — only the
  bubbles remain. A right-to-left mouse/trackpad swipe on empty space
  is the only way back.
- Each opened quote shows one of 10 minimalist line-art scenes at the
  bottom (moon, paper plane, birds, horizon, letter, mountains, candle,
  rain window, bloom, two cups). The scene is derived from the quote's
  id, so a given quote always opens with the same one.

  Reverted along the way: a cursor-parallax version of the bubbles was
  built and then removed in favour of drag-and-drop.

7. NOTES / GOTCHAS WORTH REMEMBERING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Never write glob-style text like "prefix-*" inside a CSS comment.
  A comment containing one broke the Tailwind v4 build with a confusing
  "Cannot apply unknown utility class" error on an unrelated line.
- Framer Motion maps x/y onto SVG *attributes*, which <circle> and <g>
  don't have, so those animations silently froze. The vignette scenes
  use CSS keyframes instead (the vg-* rules in index.css).
- The browser preview console often shows stale errors from earlier
  code after a reload — verify against fresh state before believing a
  regression.

9. REDESIGN - "VELVET & GILT"  (replaces the archival look entirely)
=====================================================================

WHY
- The archival version was flat: every surface sat within a couple of
  percent of black, so nothing caught light and nothing had depth. The
  Latin costume (CODICILLUS SECRETUS, FOLIO NO. I, MCMXXIV, NOTA III)
  was decoration rather than design. All of it is gone.

THE ROOM
- One warm light source above the centre, a garnet pool low-left and a
  laurel whisper low-right, over plum-black velvet, falling away into a
  vignette. The source breathes on a 14s cycle.
- Velvet nap + 5% film grain over the top so the black reads as cloth.
- The fixed corner brackets and folio register were deleted
  (ArchivalFrame.jsx removed).

PALETTE (same token names, new values - nothing else had to change)
- Ground   #060405 / #0b0709 / #150d11 / #1d1218 / #27171e / #33202a
- Garnet   #4a0c1c / #a81e3c / ember #d9584e
- Gilt     #8c6a23 / #c9a227 / #e9c972 / sheen #fff6da
- Laurel   #1a2e1f / #4c7a55 / #8fbe97
- Ink      #bca894 / #efe3d3 / #fbf5ec

TYPE
- Added Bodoni Moda (high-contrast didone) as font-title: the wordmark,
  page headings and the 404.
- Cormorant Garamond stays for anything read at length; Comfortaa stays
  for tracked micro-labels.

COMPONENTS
- Buttons are pills now. Primary is struck gold with dark ink on it -
  the only genuinely bright thing on the page - with a specular band
  that travels across on hover. Ghost is a gilt outline on velvet.
- Quote cards and floating cards: soft 20-22px radius, jewel-toned
  gradient lit from the top-left, real three-stage shadow, gilt
  hairline that brightens on hover. The chamfered plates are gone.
- Language filter: gold pill for "All", the language's own accent
  otherwise.
- Ornaments reduced to Sparkle, Pip and Rule (a gilt hairline with a
  lit diamond). Filament removed.

THE SEALED LETTER (opening a quote)
- A card now morphs into a closed envelope with a red wax seal, the
  wax swells and cracks, the flap folds back over the top edge, and
  the sheet rises out of it. ~2.3s, and skipped entirely under
  prefers-reduced-motion.
- The envelope is paper - manila #ded2b8, edge #c2b191 - and so is the
  flap. The sheet inside is warm laid paper with two fold creases.
- The writing takes the card's own colour: accentFor() gained ink and
  inkSoft (the same hue, dark enough to read on paper). The vignettes
  redraw in that ink too, via .vg-paper.

FIXES ALONG THE WAY
- The reading view is portalled to document.body. The page wrapper
  animates `filter`, which makes it a stacking context, so no z-index
  inside it could ever rise above the sticky register - the modal was
  being painted under the nav bar.
- Floating cards are clamped against their own half-width, so none is
  ever sliced by the viewport edge; on a phone the bound falls back to
  42vw and they stack down the middle.
- Route changes reset scroll to the top (inner pages were opening
  halfway down, under the register).
- The vignettes are drawn larger.

LETTER REFINEMENTS
- The wax seal is now a rose - two rings of petals round a curled heart,
  with laurel behind - and it sits on the point of the flap rather than
  above it. It swells, comes loose and falls away before the flap moves.
- Closing folds the letter back in: the sheet slides down into the
  envelope, the flap comes down over it, the colour returns, and only
  then does the envelope morph back into its card.
- The opening runs slowly and in sequence, one attribute at a time -
  letterhead, quote mark, the quote, the rule, the author, the language
  stamp, the link, then the little scene - each easing up out of a blur.
  About 5.5s end to end, collapsed to nothing under reduced motion.
- Every letter is headed "OMNIA VINCIT AMOR" (Virgil, Eclogues X) in
  small caps over a hairline - the same line on all of them.
- Once open, the envelope is rimmed in the card's own colour while the
  flap and inner frame stay paper.

- The flap's corners were square against the envelope's 20px radius and
  overhung it; they are rounded to 17px (the radius inside the 3px
  border) so the flap tucks into the corner.
- The quote now reads on the face of the sealed envelope, centred below
  the rose, and fades out as the sheet rises to take over. It comes back
  as the letter folds closed.
- Removed the large decorative opening-quote glyph from the sheet.
- The reading view is keyed per quote, so opening a different card
  remounts it rather than reusing a stale instance (previously the
  closing state could carry over and strand the letter open).

13. THE RECOMMENDATIONS ROUND  (all but deploy and git)
=====================================================================

EDIT AND DELETE AN ENTRY
- Until now delete only existed on the Add page, in the "added this
  visit" list - so anything added in an earlier session could never be
  removed or corrected from the UI at all. Both now live on the opened
  letter: Edit turns the sheet into fields (quote, author, language),
  Delete asks "Withdraw this entry?" first.
- New `updateQuote` in the store, optimistic like add and delete, and
  it keeps the previous row so a failed write is put back.
  Verified round-trip: author edited, card updated, Supabase row
  updated, then reverted.

COLOUR IS STABLE AGAIN
- Colour was keyed off array position, so adding one quote re-coloured
  the whole page and a letter you remembered as ruby came back orange.
  It is keyed off the quote id now, so a line keeps its jewel forever.
- That only works with a hash that actually spreads. The old
  `hash * 31` mix left UUIDs - which share most of their shape - in the
  same bucket. Replaced with FNV-1a plus an avalanche finalizer.
  Across the seven entries: 2 ruby, 2 orange, 2 gold, 1 yellow.

SEARCH, LANGUAGES, KEPT
- A filter bar above the grid: search across line, author and language;
  a chip per language with counts; and a "Kept" chip that shows only
  bookmarked entries, which until now were a dead end.
- Accession numbers come from a quote's place in the whole archive, so
  filtering never renumbers the plates (verified: filtering to English
  leaves 001 / 002 / 007, not 001 / 002 / 003).
- The empty state distinguishes "nothing under that heading" from
  "nothing kept yet", and offers to clear the filters.

THE LETTER CAN BE SKIPPED
- The open is a ceremony the first time and a wait the tenth. Clicking
  the letter drops straight to the end of it; every beat runs through
  at()/over() so one flag collapses the whole sequence.

PROVENANCE READS LIKE A PERSON
- Every card used to carry the identical date, which was noise rather
  than provenance. Now: KEPT TODAY / YESTERDAY / N DAYS AGO / LAST WEEK
  / N WEEKS AGO, falling back to month and year past two months.

THE ADD PAGE JOINS THE ARCHIVE
- It was still in the old vocabulary - Bodoni masthead, gold gild-face
  button, rounded-[26px] velvet panels - and read as a different site
  next to the index. Form, fields and the "added this visit" plates are
  now the archive's flat stock with jewel accents and the crimson
  action. PageHeading.jsx deleted with its last caller.

LEFT ALONE, DELIBERATELY
- Bundle is 652 KB / 194 KB gzipped, mostly Framer Motion. Fine here.
- The realtime WebSocket still fails from inside the preview pane.
  REST reads and writes are healthy, so this needs one look in an
  ordinary browser before it is treated as real.

THE WORD IS "VERSE"
- "Quote" only survived in one visible place - the crimson button - plus
  four screen-reader labels; the interface had already drifted to its
  own vocabulary. It is "verse" now throughout: Add a Verse, "Verse by
  X" on the opened letter, "Delete verse by X", "Open the X verse", and
  the edit field.
- "Line" was the other word for the same thing ("the line matters more
  than the spelling", "Search a line, a name, a language"). Both became
  verse, so there is one word rather than two.
- "Entry" is kept where it means the catalogue record rather than the
  writing: "Withdraw this entry?" on the letter.
- Code identifiers and the Supabase `quotes` table are unchanged -
  renaming a live table for a vocabulary change is churn with no
  user-visible benefit.

15. EDITING NEVER ACTUALLY SAVED
=====================================================================
Found while reading supabase-setup.sql before deploying.

- The table had select, insert and delete policies but no UPDATE one.
  Editing was added in round 13 without it, so Postgres affected zero
  rows on every edit - and PostgREST reports that as 200 with an empty
  body, not an error. supabase-js saw no error, the optimistic change
  stayed on screen, and nothing reached the database. A reload lost it.
- My verification of it in round 13 was wrong. I edited a verse, then
  reverted it, then read the row - which matches the original whether
  or not the write worked. Re-tested properly by reading the row while
  the edit was still applied.
- Fixed: added the update policy to the live database and to
  supabase-setup.sql, so a fresh project gets it too.
- Hardened so this class of bug cannot hide again: insert, update and
  delete all ask for the affected row back with .select(), and treat
  "no rows came back" as a failure - rollback plus the notice - rather
  than as success. Security advisor clean afterwards.
